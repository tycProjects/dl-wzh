Android Island 1.3.7 - Motion, Preview & Lyrics Sync

Changes:
- Replaced the abrupt iPhone/waterdrop notch shape threshold with a continuous progress-driven morph shape. The camera-cutout silhouette now morphs gradually during both expand and collapse.
- Added adjustable white lyric glow, applied only to lyric text (track title is not modified).
- Improved active playback position sampling from 250ms to 100ms in normal mode and 350ms in low-power/Ultra mode. Playback position remains based on MediaController playback timestamps.
- Added Restart Island action in settings. It cycles the enabled setting to force overlay recreation when the service is connected.
- Added media, WhatsApp, and combined notification demo scenarios accessible from settings.
- Existing Ultra Mode and HiddenApiBypass 4.3 dependency are retained.

Build status:
- ZIP integrity checked.
- Gradle compilation could not be verified in this environment because services.gradle.org DNS resolution failed.
