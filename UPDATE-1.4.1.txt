ANDROID ISLAND — UPDATE 1.4.1
Banner motion, stable capsule anchor, and WhatsApp chat stack

CHANGES
1. Banner entrance animation runs on its own spring channel. The capsule does not
   follow the banner's translation.
2. Compose overlay host has child clipping disabled, allowing the banner's entrance
   frame to render through the reserved area below the capsule without being cut by
   the composable's local bounds.
3. WhatsApp notifications are queued FIFO. New contacts wait while the active card
   remains stable; updates for the same notification refresh that entry.
4. Tapping the WhatsApp banner/capsule opens a scrollable stack of individual chat
   cards. Reply, Open, and Dismiss remain per-contact actions.
5. Pending WhatsApp count is exposed in the capsule; a green WhatsApp indicator
   remains visible beside the media capsule when media is active.
6. Per-notification expiry jobs are cancelled/replaced safely to prevent stale
   timeout jobs clearing refreshed notifications.
7. Removed an unused duplicate generic animation-spec helper.
