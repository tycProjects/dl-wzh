Android Island 1.4.11 — Expanded layer refactor

- Expanded content is now rendered as a separate panel below the fixed capsule anchor; it no longer resizes the capsule itself.
- The capsule fades fully out while expanded and returns at the same anchor as the panel closes.
- The expanded Y offset is applied to the panel only; the capsule remains at the top anchor.
- Expanded panel width/height remain independent of capsule dimensions.
- Expanded corner-radius setting clips the expanded panel.
- Reduced-motion modes disable lyric shine while retaining the primary open/close transition.
- Overlay width is stable during each transition and remains compact during ordinary compact playback.

Validation note: source-level checks are run in this package. A successful Android/Gradle compile and on-device behavior test are not claimed unless separately reported.
