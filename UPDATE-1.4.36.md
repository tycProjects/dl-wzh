# Android Island 1.4.36

- Horizontal swipe on the media capsule changes tracks only: swipe left = next, swipe right = previous.
- The capsule itself no longer translates or follows the finger during track navigation; its X/Y anchor stays fixed.
- WhatsApp/banner surfaces do not receive the media track-swipe gesture.
- Vertical swipe and configured tap/double-tap/long-press actions remain unchanged.
