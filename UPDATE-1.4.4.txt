Android Island 1.4.4

Fixes (proven against remaining 1.4.3 bugs):

1. Capsule fixed position
   - COMPACT and BANNER now share one full-width scene (screen width).
   - WindowManager WRAP_CONTENT width no longer changes when a banner appears,
     so the capsule never re-centers or shifts by even 1px.
   - Capsule is placed with an absolute offset inside that scene; banner is a
     separate sibling layer.

2. WhatsApp indicator
   - Defaults to the LEFT of the capsule.
   - Toggle in settings (left / right) is preserved and applied in both
     COMPACT and BANNER states.

3. Banner swipe dismiss no longer clipped
   - Parent scene is full-width → horizontal travel has room.
   - Travel limit raised to ~55% of banner width.
   - Scale + alpha track the finger continuously so the card does not get
     cut by window bounds while swiping either direction.

4. Swipe up → tuck
   - Swipe up on the banner collapses to COMPACT while keeping the unread
     item in the queue (collapseWhatsAppBanner). Tap capsule to reopen.

5. Smoother motion
   - IslandMotion springs re-tuned (lower base stiffness, higher damping)
     to reduce stutter on both high and low refresh-rate panels.

Reference: interaction concepts from EvanKoe/expressive-cutout (GPL-3.0)
and other public Dynamic Island Android projects. No upstream source files
were copied; implementation stays on this project's Compose architecture.
