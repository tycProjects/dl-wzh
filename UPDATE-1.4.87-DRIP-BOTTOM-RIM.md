# Update 1.4.87 — Drip moved to the bottom, rim on all edges

- The drip film no longer sits on the control row. A thin liquid rim now runs along every edge of
  the glass.
- The bottom band is thicker: liquid tongues rise from the bottom edge into the card, each ending in
  a bead with a glint, and the liquid is denser toward the bottom.
- Tongues are spread evenly across the width; the old cluster on the right side is gone.
- Sliders are unchanged: "Efek netes (drip)" sets strength (0% = off), "Panjang tetesan" sets length.

Known limits:
- Not compiled or run on a device here. Test on Android 13+ (AGSL). Older Android uses a gradient band.
