# Update 1.4.88 — Upward tongues removed

- The tongues that rose from the bottom edge into the card, and their beads and glints, are removed.
- What remains: a thin liquid rim along every edge, and a thicker liquid band along the bottom that is
  denser toward the bottom edge.
- Sliders are unchanged: "Efek netes (drip)" sets strength, and "Panjang tetesan" now sets the band
  thickness.

Known limits:
- Not compiled or run on a device here. Test on Android 13+ (AGSL). Older Android uses a gradient band.
- Drips hanging out below the card still need extra window space, which is not added yet.
