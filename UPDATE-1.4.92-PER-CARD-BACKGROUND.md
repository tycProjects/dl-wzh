# Update 1.4.92 — One background per WhatsApp card

- The WhatsApp stack no longer draws one shared background. Each card has its own glass surface,
  its own background from its chat's avatar, and its own effects.
- A chat without an avatar uses the WhatsApp icon as its background.
- The stack padding and gap sliders still apply between cards.

Known limits:
- Not compiled or run on a device here. Test with two or more chats that have different avatars.
