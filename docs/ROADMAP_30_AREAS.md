## Development milestones

**M1 — Playable foundation:** current source, subject to Android build validation.  
**M2 — Family and sandbox:** existing broad fields and relationship graph; continue complete histories, validation, more derived relatives.  
**M3 — Sandbox-Dynasty:** existing living-relative switching; implement inheritance/property transfer, managed minor decisions, fuller tree UI.  
**M4 — Integrated life systems:** deepen education, wages, taxes, multiple jobs, health, family, economics and side businesses.  
**M5 — Global content and systemic expansion:** geography, historic eras, politics, law, military, sports, fame, religion, world events, challenge gameplay and localization.  
**M6 — Quality assurance:** signed builds, long-run performance, Android compatibility, accessibility, reliable migrations, complete backup failure testing.

## Access policy separation

**Temporary testing:** everything that actually exists is free. No payment code or stub premium dialogs.  
**Final commercial design (not activated):** free core game plus basic Sandbox; optional one-time permanent advanced sandbox family-editing and Sandbox-Dynasty purchase, no subscription, with restoration and eligible offline access. No pay-to-win in achievement-eligible modes.
