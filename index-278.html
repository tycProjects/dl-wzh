    How to zip your project
    
      
    Most Android zips just work. Here's what happens, and what to avoid.
    
      
      
        
    • 
          
      1
      
          
      Nesting depth doesn't matter. We find settings.gradle wherever it is.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      2
      
          
      Wrapper optional. Missing? We add one. Have one? We use it as-is.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      3
      
          
      local.properties is removed so it can't break the build.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      4
      
          
      Custom CI workflow? We use yours if present, or generate one.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      5
      
          
      Build output is skipped: build/, .gradle/, *.apk. We rebuild it fresh.
      
        
      • 
      
    

      
    

      
    
        
    
          Works fine
          MyApp.zip
    └ MyApp/
      ├ settings.gradle
      ├ build.gradle
      └ app/
    …nested further, wrapper or no wrapper.
        
    
        
    
          Avoid
          No settings.gradle in the zip: the one thing that can't be auto-detected.
        
    
      
    

      
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
      
    Privacy Policy
    
      
    Last updated August 29, 2026. What Tycept collects when you use WZH 2 APK, and why.
    
      
      
        
    • 
          
      1
      
          
      What gets sent to our servers. The zip, link, or code you submit is uploaded automatically to build your APK, along with basic technical data (IP address, device/browser type, timestamps) needed to run and secure the build service.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      2
      
          
      Why we use it. Solely to run your build, show progress, generate your APK and signing key, and keep your build history on this device.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      3
      
          
      What we don't do. We don't sell your data, and we don't inspect uploaded source beyond what's needed to build it.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      4
      
          
      Storage & retention. Build artifacts and logs are kept only as long as needed to let you download them, then removed on a rolling basis. Signing keys are yours - download and back them up yourself.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      5
      
          
      Security. Uploaded projects and build data are transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted to what's needed to run your build. No system is 100% breach-proof; if we ever discover unauthorized access to your data, we'll notify affected users as required by law.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      6
      
          
      Local storage. Your device also keeps small bits of data locally (like your build history and a device ID) to make the app work across visits. This isn't shared with anyone and you can clear it anytime from the History tab or your browser settings.
      
        
      • 
        
    • 
          
      7
      
          
      Your choices. You can clear your local build history anytime from the History tab. To request deletion of anything held server-side, contact us.
