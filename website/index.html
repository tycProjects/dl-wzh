Security & Cryptography Protocol

Mathematical specifications and cross-platform compatibility.

1. True AES-256-GCM: Our military mode uses 10,000 PBKDF2 derivation iterations with HMAC-SHA-256, generating a 256-bit AES key. Each message includes a unique 16-byte random salt and 12-byte nonce (IV) with a 128-bit authentication tag.


2. Emoji & Cipher Substitution: Emoji encoding maps UTF-8 bytes to our 16-element cyber emoji alphabet. It is designed for visual disguise and casual concealment across chat apps.


3. 100% Offline: All operations execute purely client-side inside your browser or device. No server requests, no telemetry, no tracking.
